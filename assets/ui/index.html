Welcome to Fukidashi

Fukidashi reads the Japanese text on screen and draws its translation over it, where the text was. First, choose where the text is translated: nothing is downloaded until you do.

On this computer

A local model: private, free, and it works offline. It is loaded with the first translation and unloaded after a while without one.

…
…

With a provider

An OpenAI-compatible service (OpenRouter, a server on another machine): nothing to download. Only the text of the screen is sent, never the image.
